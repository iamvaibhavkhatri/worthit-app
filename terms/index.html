Worth It? Pro

Most of Worth It? is free. Worth It? Pro is optional and comes in two plans:

  • Pro Yearly is an auto-renewing subscription. The price for the first year and for each renewal is shown in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
  • Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no renewals.

A subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple ID is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not refund the current period.

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone, tap Restore purchases in the app's You tab to get Pro back.

Not financial advice

Worth It? shows prices as hours of your work time to help you think about purchases. It does not give financial, tax or investment advice. Its numbers are estimates based on the pay you enter, and decisions are always yours.

Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email vaibhavkhatri14@gmail.com. You can also tap Share feedback in the app's Settings, which fills in your app and iOS version for you.

How do I get Pro back on a new iPhone?

In the app's Settings, tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel Pro Yearly?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, choose Worth It?, and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I get a salary, not an hourly wage. Does it work?

Yes. Enter your pay the way you get it, and Worth It? works out what an hour of your time is worth.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for Worth It?, and pick a size.

Is my data backed up?

Your data stays on your iPhone and is included in your normal iCloud or computer backups. Worth It? has no account or server of its own.

Worth It? Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version: Worth It? does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Your pay details, the prices you check, your skips and buys, your goals and your settings are saved only on your device. The app shares a small summary (such as your hours kept) with its own widgets through storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Worth It? has no servers.

Photos

If you add a cover photo to a goal, you pick it with Apple's photo picker. The app receives only the photo you choose, stores a copy on your device, and cannot see the rest of your library.

Tock's lines

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Tock's lines can be reworded by Apple's on-device model. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Purchases

Worth It? Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple may give developers anonymous sales reports.

App Store analytics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as download counts and crash reports. These cannot be used to identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Tracking and advertising

Worth It? contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Emailing us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we never share it.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. Device backups you make with iCloud or your computer may include it, as with any app, and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Worth It? is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

The problem

Most impulse buys happen in a few seconds: a late-night scroll, a countdown timer, "only 3 left". Prices on a screen feel small, and the "was it worth it?" only comes days later.

Budget apps ask for bank logins, categories and spreadsheets. Most people don't need more admin. They need a moment to think, in a number that means something to them.

How Worth It? helps

  1. Check the priceSee it as time: hours and minutes of your own work.
  2. DecideSkip it or buy it. There's no wrong answer, and Tock never judges.
  3. Keep the hoursEvery skip adds to your hours kept and moves your goals closer.

Meet Tock

Tock is a little clock who keeps you company. Tock reacts to every check, celebrates your skips, is happy for you when you buy something you love, and shape-shifts into a balloon, a rocket or a trophy when you reach a milestone. Calm, warm and a bit silly. Never a lecture.
